In this repo "C:\Users\lauro_4cudofq\Documents\dev\GO2_celta" we have successfuly automated the creation of content. It isnt 100% completed but we have an mvp. the repo creates content based on seeds and a framework, creates exercises, compiles it and creates json and html files. json are used to upload content to a plataform via an API and the handoff is currently functional. 
why do I say this... because we need a similar pipe for our "Global Leadership" program.
Global Leadership program is much different to GO2 because the length of the program in GO2 is predetermined, whereas programs in Global Leadership programs can vary. Also audiances are different, since this is the automated pipe for soft skills developpment we need to consider our target audiance: junior, middle or upper management, because what we create as content will require different output. Upper management might not see the problems the same way a junior management will.

Our pipe needs to create 
- syllabus (adaptable to the amount of hours, sessions required)
- for each unit or module of the syllabus 
    - 1 investigation article (ex:examples\insulet\content\INSULET-T01-ART01-A.md)
    - 1 opinión article (ex: examples\insulet\content\INSULET-T01-ART02-A.md)
    - 1 "personality quizz" (look at workshop-links and googlesheet-appsscript for more details)
    - 1 case study
    - 1 "committment letter" in which participants commit to making changes based on the softskills taught (said commitment letter must be in google forms as well; the results require analysis and feedback to be returned to the sender)
