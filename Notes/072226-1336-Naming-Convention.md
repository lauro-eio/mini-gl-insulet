Naming convention

All local notes under `Notes/` use this prefix and a `.md` extension:

`MMDDYY-HHMM-<slug>.md`

- Date: month, day, two-digit year with no separators (US order)
- Time: 24-hour clock, hours then minutes (no colon)
- Slug: short topic label (prefer a real topic over bare `note`)

Example:

`072226-1336-naming-convention.md`

→ created on July 22, 2026, at 08:14, about naming conventions.

Notes in this folder are gitignored and stay on this machine only.
