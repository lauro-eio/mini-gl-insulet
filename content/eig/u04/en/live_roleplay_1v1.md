# Role-Play 1v1 — Taking the Reset Before Responding to a Peer Who Wants an Instant Answer

**Duration:** 25–35 minutes
**Purpose:** Practice running a breath, ground, or reframe move on a high-intensity moment without freezing the floor or losing a peer's trust.

## Roles

**Role 1 — Frontline supervisor (Regulation Owner)**
You have felt a strong surge—anger, fear, or overwhelm—tied to a moment the team is watching. You want ten to twenty seconds to run one regulation move before you respond. You must not skip the move just to avoid friction, and you must not turn it into an open-ended delay.

**Role 2 — Skeptical peer supervisor**
You are under the same pressure and believe responses on the floor should be immediate—"say something, move on." You see the pause as overthinking or stalling. You will back off only if the Regulation Owner names the specific move they are running and gives a concrete, short time box for it.

## Conflict

Can the Regulation Owner secure agreement for a short, named regulation move on one specific moment without the peer escalating over their head or the tension curdling into a grudge?

## Facilitator notes

- Round 1 (8 min): Peer pushes for an immediate response; Regulation Owner runs a move out loud.
- Round 2 (8 min): Regulation Owner names the specific move (breathe, ground, or reframe) and proposes a time-boxed pause for it.
- Debrief (10 min): Where did the Regulation Owner nearly skip the move under pressure? What language actually landed with the skeptical peer?
