# Framework — The Reset Card: Breathe, Ground, Reframe Before You Respond

Use in workshop or print for the floor. Run this card any time you feel a surge that has a response attached to it—before you speak, radio, or send the message.

## 1. The moment (one sentence)

What just happened, and what am I feeling right now?

## 2. Pick the move

| Situation | Best first move |
| --- | --- |
| Body is flooded (tight chest, racing pulse, multiple alarms) | Breathe |
| Mind is spiraling faster than you can think straight | Ground |
| A story is attached ("they did this to me / on purpose") | Reframe |

## 3. Run the move

- **Breathe:** Inhale 4 seconds, exhale 6 seconds. Repeat twice. Longer exhale than inhale, every time.
- **Ground:** Name one thing you can feel underfoot or in your hand, one thing you can hear, one thing you can see right now.
- **Reframe:** Finish this sentence before you respond: "A more useful way to read this moment is ___."

## 4. Say the regulated version

What do you say now that is different from what you almost said before running the move?

## 5. Visibility

Where does the team see that you took the beat—a pause, a breath, a phrase, a hand on the counter?
