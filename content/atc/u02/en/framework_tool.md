# Framework — Team Identity Canvas: Daily Votes

Use in workshop or print for the floor. Complete one canvas per identity you want your team to build this month.

## 1. Aspirational identity

"We are the kind of team that ________."

## 2. Evidence audit

| Question | Your notes |
| --- | --- |
| What already proves this identity is true? | |
| What contradicts it right now (a shortcut, a padded number, a skipped step)? | |

## 3. Today's vote

What one small, visible action this shift counts as evidence for the identity? (Under 5 minutes, done by the team, not just described.)

## 4. Layers check

| Layer | Your design |
| --- | --- |
| Outcome (the result we want) | |
| Process (the step that produces it) | |
| Identity (the sentence the process proves) | |

## 5. Visibility

Where does the team see or hear the identity reinforced (stand-up line, board, handover note)?

## 6. Onboarding a newcomer

How will a new hire learn this identity in week one—who narrates it, and what do they watch, not just read?

## 7. Pressure test

Name one situation this month (rush order, bad shift, short-staffing) likely to test this identity. What will you coach the team to protect first?

## 8. Friday review

Keep / tweak / drop the vote—and one sentence for your manager describing the identity in outcome language, not soft language.
