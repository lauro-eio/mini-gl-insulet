# Framework — Narrative Integrity & Memory-Check Canvas

Use in a debrief or incident review, before the room's story is treated as final.

## 1. The event (one sentence)

What are we debriefing or reviewing, right now?

## 3. Memory-check

- **Hindsight check:** Does any part of the story sound "obvious" only because we now know how it ended?
- **Outcome check:** Are we rating the decision on the reasoning available at the time, or on the result?
- **Peak-end check:** Is this account weighted by the most dramatic moment or the ending, more than the full record?

## 4. Gap

Where does the room's story diverge from the record above? Name the gap plainly.

## 5. Credit

Who actually caught, flagged, or acted first, per the record — not per who tells the story loudest?
