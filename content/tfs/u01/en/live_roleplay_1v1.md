# Role-Play 1v1 — Defending a Pause to a Peer Who Wants Speed

**Duration:** 25–35 minutes
**Purpose:** Practice protecting a brief structured check on a high-stakes or unfamiliar call without freezing the floor or losing a peer's trust.

## Roles

**Role 1 — Frontline supervisor (Pause Owner)**
You have flagged a call as novel, high-stakes, or conflicting with the data, and you want a short check before committing. You must not skip the check just to avoid friction, and you must not turn it into an open-ended delay.

**Role 2 — Skeptical peer supervisor**
You are under the same deadline and believe "we've done this a hundred times, just call it." You push for the fast path and treat the pause as second-guessing. You will back off only if the Pause Owner names a specific trigger and gives a concrete time-boxed check.

## Conflict

Can the Pause Owner secure agreement for a two-to-four-minute check on one specific call without the peer escalating over their head or the deadline slipping past recovery?

## Facilitator notes

- Round 1 (8 min): Peer pushes for the fast call; Pause Owner responds.
- Round 2 (8 min): Pause Owner names the specific trigger (novelty, stakes, or conflict) and proposes a time-boxed check.
- Debrief (10 min): Where did the Pause Owner nearly fold under pressure? What language actually landed with the skeptical peer?
