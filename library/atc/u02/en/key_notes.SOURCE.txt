Unit Goal Statement:
• This unit equipped you to transform your self-image, empowering every small action to become a vote for the breakthrough identity you aspire to.

Core Learning Objectives:
• Understand the three layers of behavior change—outcomes, processes, and identity.
• Distinguish between outcome-based and identity-based habits.
• Identify how each small win reinforces your desired self-image.
• Recognize and leverage the feedback loop that solidifies your new identity through consistent actions.

Implementation Checklist:
1. Draft your personal Aspirational Identity Statement by defining the person you want to be.
2. List three key daily habits that directly support your new identity.
3. Map your current routines to identify opportunities where small 1% improvements can be integrated.
4. Set up a simple tracking system (journal, app, or spreadsheet) to record each small win that reinforces your self-image.
5. Optimize your immediate environment—remove obstacles and introduce cues that remind you of your new identity.
6. Schedule a weekly self-reflection session to evaluate progress, celebrate small successes, and recalibrate if needed.
7. Share your Aspirational Identity Statement and progress with a trusted peer for accountability and feedback.

Reflection Prompt:
• In what ways does each small, intentional action today prove that you are becoming the person you aspire to be, and how can you leverage this insight to overcome challenges tomorrow?
