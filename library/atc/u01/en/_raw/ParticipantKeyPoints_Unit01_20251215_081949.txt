Unit Goal Statement:
• This unit equipped you to harness the power of atomic improvements—transforming big goals into daily, compounding actions that drive breakthrough results.

Core Learning Objectives:
• Understand the 1% improvement mindset and recognize how tiny, daily changes compound into monumental achievements.  
• Break down major goals into manageable, actionable steps for sustained progress.  
• Design and implement a robust system that replaces sporadic efforts with consistent habit-driven routines.  
• Apply the habit loop framework to form new, empowering habits and phase out counterproductive ones.

Implementation Checklist:
1. Identify one major long-term goal and list 3–5 tiny, actionable steps (each representing a 1% improvement) that directly contribute to it.
2. Audit your current routines—pinpoint one habit to improve and specify a daily action that nudges it in the right direction.
3. Build your habit loop: Clearly define a cue, a simple response, an associated craving, and a rewarding outcome for your chosen behavior.
4. Design a daily or weekly schedule that embeds these atomic actions into your routine, ensuring the changes are both visible and trackable.
5. Set up a simple system (such as a log or journal) to measure, review, and refine your progress regularly.
6. Create a supportive environment: Organize your physical and digital spaces to minimize friction for the new habits, reinforcing the desired behavior.
7. Commit to a weekly progress review session where you assess the impact of these small changes and adjust your systems as needed.

Reflection Prompt:
• How can you redesign your daily routine to consistently leverage the power of tiny improvements, and what specific adjustments will ensure that these changes compound into lasting breakthrough results?
