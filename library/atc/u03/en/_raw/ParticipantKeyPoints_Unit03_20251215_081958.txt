Unit Goal Statement:
"This unit equipped you to master the habit loop, enabling you to build empowering routines and dismantle counterproductive patterns."

Core Learning Objectives:
• Clearly explain each stage of the habit loop (cue, craving, response, reward).  
• Identify and analyze your personal triggers for unwanted behaviors.  
• Apply strategies to replace detrimental habits with positive, goal-aligned actions.  
• Develop a customized plan to track and measure subtle daily improvements.

Implementation Checklist:
1. Document one specific habit loop by noting its cue, craving, response, and reward.  
2. Identify three recurring cues that lead to an unwanted behavior and record the context in which they occur.  
3. Create a targeted intervention plan by selecting one negative habit and outlining a practical alternative response.  
4. Establish a daily log to record instances of both negative and positive habit cycles for ongoing analysis.  
5. Make one change in your environment that supports the formation of your new, positive habit loop.  
6. Share your intervention plan with an accountability partner to boost commitment and gather feedback.

Reflection Prompt:
"What is the most impactful habit loop change you can commit to right now, and how will transforming this loop drive your overall progress?"
