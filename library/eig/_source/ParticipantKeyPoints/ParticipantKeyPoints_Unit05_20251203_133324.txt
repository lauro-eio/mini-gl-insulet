Unit Goal Statement:
"This unit equipped you to transform your interpersonal interactions by harnessing empathy and refining your social communication skills for deeper, more impactful connections."

Core Learning Objectives:
• Recognize empathy as the essential bridge to enhancing interpersonal relationships.
• Understand the neurological basis behind empathetic responses.
• Demonstrate active and reflective listening techniques in real-life conversations.
• Master effective verbal and non-verbal communication strategies to foster social intelligence.

Implementation Checklist:
1. Review a recent conversation and list specific moments where empathy was naturally expressed.
2. Identify one instance where empathetic listening could have changed the outcome and plan how to apply reflective listening next time.
3. Practice active listening with a peer by maintaining eye contact and summarizing their points in a follow-up conversation.
4. Record a short note on your feelings during an interaction, focusing on non-verbal cues that either supported or hindered communication.
5. Develop three actionable strategies to improve your verbal and non-verbal cues in daily interactions.
6. Engage in a role-play with a colleague or friend to experiment with different methods of empathetic engagement.
7. Schedule a check-in next week to assess how these new strategies impacted your communication and relationships.

Reflection Prompt:
How have your conscious efforts to practice empathy and refine your social cues altered the dynamics of your personal or professional interactions, and what insights have you gained about yourself in the process?
