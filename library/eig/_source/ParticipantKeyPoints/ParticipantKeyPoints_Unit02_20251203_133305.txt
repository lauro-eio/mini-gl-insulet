Unit Goal Statement: This unit equipped you to swiftly identify and harness your brain's fast, automatic emotional responses to make empowered, balanced decisions.

Core Learning Objectives:
• Understand key neural circuits, including the roles of the amygdala and prefrontal cortex, in emotional processing.  
• Recognize the specific triggers that lead to emotional hijackings.  
• Identify when instinctive reactions override rational thought.  
• Learn practical strategies for regulating impulsive emotional responses.

Implementation Checklist:
1. Review your notes on the amygdala’s role and map out two common scenarios where emotional hijackings occur.  
2. List your top three personal triggers and annotate what immediate emotions they spark.  
3. Practice a pause-and-reflect exercise by taking a deep breath and counting slowly to five whenever you recognize a trigger.  
4. Record a brief log of today’s instances where you successfully interrupted an impulsive reaction using your new strategy.  
5. Identify one upcoming situation in your schedule where you can pre-plan an emotional regulation tactic.  
6. Commit to a weekly check-in to assess progress and adjust your approach based on real-world feedback.

Reflection Prompt:
How can you transform your instinctive emotional signals into a valuable cue for cultivating better decision-making in high-pressure moments?
