ASSESSMENT DESIGN BLUEPRINT

I. Assessment Dimensions  
 1. Evolutionary & Neural Insight  
  • Measures understanding of the evolutionary origins of emotions and the neural mechanisms (limbic system, prefrontal cortex) driving emotional responses.  
 2. Cultivating Self-Awareness  
  • Evaluates participants’ ability to recognize, label, and reflect on their emotional states through mindfulness and self-reflection practices.  
 3. Mastering Emotional Regulation  
  • Assesses proficiency in implementing techniques for managing and moderating emotional responses, including real-time regulation methods and cognitive reappraisal.  
 4. Empathy & Social Intelligence  
  • Gauges the development of empathic engagement and effective social communication skills, emphasizing active listening and non-verbal expression.
