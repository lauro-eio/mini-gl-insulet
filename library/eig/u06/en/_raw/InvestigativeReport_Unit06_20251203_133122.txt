One anonymous source within the company stated, “Our meetings have become echo chambers of ignored emotions. We are consistently pushed to overlook the very principles designed to help us manage stress and resolve conflict intelligently.” This systemic failure to operationalize emotional intelligence did not just affect individual interactions; it also corrupted the broader corporate culture.

From an operational standpoint, the issues extend into workplace and leadership dynamics, as specified in sections 6.2.1 and 6.2.2. The internal review infamously noted a “systemic undervaluation of emotional literacy,” which had transformed a once vibrant, innovative culture into a battleground for competing interests and unmet expectations.
